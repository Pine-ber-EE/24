How to play two player 24:

• Look at the card! There should be four numbers.
• Use addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to create the number 24!
• You need to use all four numbers, and exactly once each.
• When a player figures out the solution, press their respective side of the shift key
• Player 1 is left shift player 2 is right shift.
• A timer appear will counting down from 5 seconds.
• The player must clearly explain the a correct solution before the timer runs out to get a point.
• If the player fails to do so, the opposing player is awarded the point instead.
• Press the spacebar one finished explaining to stop the timer and earn a point
• The faster you go the faster the timer will count down! Good luck!!
