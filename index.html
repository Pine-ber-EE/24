How to play two player 24:

There are two teams, yellow and green.

Take the numbers on the card and use addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division to create the number 24!

Once a team figures out a solution, press their side of the shift button (yellow = left shift, green = right shift).

They have five seconds to explain their solution. If they suceed, they keep the card. If they fail, the other team gets the card.

Whoever has the most cards at the end of the round wins!
